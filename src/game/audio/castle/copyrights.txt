Original synthesized castle transformation audio
Local basenames: transition, transitionRelease (WAV and Ogg copies).
Created for this project, 2026-11-12. No third-party recordings or samples.
transition: an eight-second airy wind and irregular sparkling tones over a soft
harmonic bed. transitionRelease: a related upward shimmer and resolving chord,
with a 1.65-second tail. Synthesized tones and noise; no sampled melodies.
48 kHz mono PCM WAV masters and Ogg Vorbis copies.
Playback sustains through generation and resolves as the mist flows away.
