CogFireStudios — Big Scary Portal Open
Local basenames: transition, transitionRelease (each with WAV and Ogg copies)
Source and CC0 declaration: https://freesound.org/people/CogFireStudios/sounds/617761/
Downloaded public HQ preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/617/617761_7614679-hq.mp3
Original title: Big Scary Portal Open. Original publication: 2022-02-06.
Retrieved: 2026-10-28. CC0 1.0 Universal; attribution is not required.
The public MP3 preview was used; the original WAV was not downloaded.
Adaptation: lowered pitch/sample rate to 75%, resampled to 48 kHz.
transition: slowed fourfold, trimmed to 20 seconds, 35 Hz high-pass / 1800 Hz
low-pass, then a two-second crossfade to form an eighteen-second energy loop.
transitionRelease: first 1.6 seconds reversed, filtered at 35–2500 Hz and faded.
Both clips have gain adjustment and are mono PCM WAV / nominal 192 kbps Vorbis.
Playback loops for the actual effect duration; volume follows the mist's fades.
