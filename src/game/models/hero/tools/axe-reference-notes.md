# Axe shape and action references

Viewed 2026-10-23; reference media is not distributed with the model.

- [Hultafors forest axe](https://hultafors.com/en-gb/collections/outdoor/products/forest-axe): a curved wooden haft, narrow steel eye, flat poll, broad single cutting bit, and a convex sharpened edge. The 500/650 mm reference handle lengths informed the scale; the game version uses slightly exaggerated head width for readability.
- [Hultafors Dvardala profile photograph](https://www.tomsoutdoors.com.au/products/hultafors-dvardala-hunting-forest-axe): inspected the continuous curve of the handle, the beard below the blade, the narrowing cheek, the thin polished edge and the wood protruding through the eye. This is a shape reference, not a copied texture or branded replica.

The asset must read as an axe from its silhouette: one wedge-shaped bit, a short poll and a curved haft. A rectangular paddle, a double-ended hammer head, and a stretched pole are rejected.

The action uses a slower anticipation, a short accelerating strike with the bit leading, a brief impact pause, a follow-through, and withdrawal clear of the wood before lowering. Arms, torso and knees participate. The right hand holds the short haft throughout the cycle; the spare hand stays clear beside the body. Blade height alone does not establish a successful visual result.


Motion revision, 2026-10-23:

- [USDA Forest Service: Using an Ax, chopping styles](https://www.fs.usda.gov/t-d/pubs/pdfpubs/pdf18232812P/Part11a_UsingAnAx.pdf) and [National Sawyer Training, axe basics](https://www.pcta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/MOD04-Axe-Basics-Maint-and-Use-IG-Final.pdf): shoulder-led diagonal strikes for standing timber, an unrushed wind-up, a planted stance, bent and extended elbows, and attention to the impact point. Applied as stylized motion guidance, while retaining the requested left-to-right gameplay sweep.
- [An Ax to Grind, Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uYNHWH6ipic): found the Forest Service companion video, but seeking stalled in browser playback; do not count the video as a watched reference.
- The current haft is 1.24 authored units. The hero targets the exposed canopy face and repositions between strokes as its extent shrinks. Full-mesh clearance checks include the handle butt, head, sleeves, torso and legs, plus the uncut vegetation rows.
