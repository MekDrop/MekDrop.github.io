in the Clairvoyance generated pictures folder. The mockup establishes the tall
three-candle brass stands, small matching tabletop stand, and feather quill in
an ink pot. Existing throne, banners, benches, globe, scrolls and books retain
their original game models.

The new props are locally authored Blender models; no Tripo credits were used.
Each adjacent `.blend` is the editable source of truth. The floor stand has a
wide stepped base, tapered neck, slender shaft, connected ascending arms,
three drip cups, ivory candles and named wick attachment points. Its smaller
tabletop companion shares the silhouette. The writing set contains an
octagonal ink bottle, visible opening and inserted faceted feather quill.
Hidden surfaces are inferred as rotationally symmetric metalwork and a closed
base. Models have bottom-center placement and export with glTF Y up.

Measured vertex bounds (metres): floor stand 0.540 × 1.615 × 0.360;
table stand 0.108 × 0.323 × 0.072; writing set 0.122 × 0.291 × 0.090.
The writing set's asymmetric feather extends 0.077 m toward local +X;
its reserved 0.18 m footprint includes that offset. All three are static,
unskinned, uncompressed meshes with flat shading and plain PBR materials.
Flames remain runtime effects attached to the authored wick empties.
